Final Conclusion

The quantization results show a clear tradeoff: increasing KVCLIP_CACHE_COMPRESSION_QUANT improves cache compression and space savings, but also increases numerical error in the KV tensor.

For BF16 KV cache, QUANT=2 is the best practical balance. It gives meaningful compression improvement while keeping tensor error low: 0.842% mean percentage error, 2.29% P99 percentage error, and 0.99997777 cosine similarity.

QUANT=4 gives stronger density but higher quality risk. QUANT=8 saved about ~95% in the capacity benchmark, but tensor error has not been measured yet.

Recommended default: use KVCLIP_CACHE_COMPRESSION_QUANT=2 for BF16 KV cache. Use QUANT=4 only when cache capacity is more important than fidelity.
